The Challenge at Traditional Mandis

Why traditional first-come-first-served procurement queues harm smallholder farmers.

Hours of Idle Waiting

Farmers often park tractor-trolleys overnight in extreme heat or rain without knowing whether their produce will be procured today or carried over.

Mandi Yard Congestion

Unplanned simultaneous arrival of hundreds of vehicles leads to severe traffic jams at weighbridges, moisture testing labs, and bag unloading zones.

Lack of Turn Transparency

Manual paper slips create uncertainty, disputes over queue order, and difficulty for operators managing daily procurement targets.

Core Innovation

Machine Learning Queue Estimation

Unlike static clock estimates, AgriQueue employs a trained scikit-learn RandomForestRegressor that predicts the exact waiting time until your procurement service begins.

  • Dynamic Queue Workload: Accounts for live farmers ahead and total tonnage in the yard.
  • Crop-Specific Complexity: Oilseeds (Soybean/Mustard) take longer due to oil & moisture laboratory tests than grains.
  • Multi-Counter Concurrency: Models service distribution across 1 to 4 active mandi counters.
  • Fail-Safe Architecture: If the ML model is offline, a calibrated heuristic fallback immediately steps in without application downtime.
